Questions

Frequently Asked

What is included in the setup fee? +

The setup fee covers domain configuration, design customization, content upload (photos, hours, description), Google Maps integration, basic SEO optimization, and deployment to a fast CDN.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your monthly plan at any time — no questions asked. Your directory listing remains active.

What if I want to update my website content? +

Pro and Growth plans include a simple editor where you can update text, photos, hours, and menu. Starter plan includes 2 free content updates per month by request.

Do I need a custom domain? +

Starter and Pro plans use a subdomain (yourcafe.{{CONFIG_FULL_DOMAIN}}). Growth plan includes a custom domain of your choice (e.g. yourcafe.com) — or you can bring your own.
